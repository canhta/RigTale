# Fixture Risk-to-Requirement Matrix

**Owner:** `SPIKE-F001` (`RGT-S003`). Method steps 1 and 2.

**Purpose.** Map every material production risk to at least one isolated fixture assertion, before any asset or adapter is written. `SPIKE-F001` states the reason: a convenient demo scene can make a weak architecture look successful. A risk with no assertion is a stop condition, not an omission.

**69 risks.** They are derived from the charter objectives, constraints and Release 1 scope; the v1 requirements; the seven quality gates and the quality dimensions they check; the published fixture cast in `fixtures/`; and the accepted findings of `RGT-S001`, `RGT-S009` and `RGT-S014`. Each row names where the risk is evidenced. A requirement is never cited as evidence for the risk it endangers: where a risk follows from a requirement by design rather than from a finding, the row says so in those words.

**Fixture layer** is one of `contract` (small valid and invalid artifacts), `diagnostic` (short shot isolating one behaviour), `production` (the complete 150–210 second run), or `rule` (audited by reading the fixture and its version history, not by executing it). Short diagnostic output cannot replace full-duration evidence, and a `rule` is never counted as coverage for a requirement.
